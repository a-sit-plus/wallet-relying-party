POTENTIAL Use Cases

Login for a legal entity based on a power of representation.
Authenticate with wallet credentials to open a bank account.
Authenticate with wallet credentials to register a SIM card.
Present a mobile driving license to an online service (remote use case).
Present a PID to authorize access to a signature key at a QTSP.
Present a Health ID associated with the wallet holder to the service provider.

Generic Use Cases

Request any custom combination of data.
Simple request that skips the consent screen at the online wallet.
Customers authenticate via their wallet at a counter. Employees are presented with all identity data of recent authentication results, which can be used to compare whether the right person is standing in front of the counter.
